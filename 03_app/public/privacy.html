Privacy

Sketcheat is a student project (IAAC, Master in AI for Architecture, Team 4), made for a one-week hackathon. Last updated: 19 November 2026.

1Who we are

Team 4, IAAC Barcelona. Contact: hetashri.rajendrakumar.kalyani@students.iaac.net

2What data we keep

  • A small copy (200 px) of the sketch photo you upload.
  • What you asked for: area, number of bedrooms, balcony or not.
  • The layouts the model made, and the layouts you save (plan, score, DXF file).
  • Your account: email address and password. The password is stored only as a hash by Supabase, never by us in readable form.
  • Each design and saved layout is linked to your account, so only you can see it.
  • Two login cookies, so the app knows you are logged in. No tracking or analytics cookies, so no cookie banner.

We do not ask for your name. Please do not draw personal information on your sketch.

3Why

To make floor plan options from your sketch and to let you save and download them. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in running a course demo, and your choice to upload a sketch.

4Who else handles it

ServiceWhat it does
CloudflareHosts the app and stores your designs and saved layouts (D1 database).
SupabaseKeeps the accounts (email and password hash) and checks your login.
RenderRuns our layout model. Your full sketch photo is sent there to make the layouts.

5Where it is stored

Everything stays in the EU: designs in Cloudflare D1 (Western Europe), accounts in Supabase (Ireland), and the model server on Render in Frankfurt.

6How long

Saved layouts stay until you delete them in the gallery. Your account stays until you ask us to delete it. All data is deleted when the course project ends (by 31 January 2027).

7Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, delete or export your data, and withdraw your consent. Write to the contact email above; we answer within one month. You can delete a saved layout yourself in the gallery. To delete your whole account and all its data, write to the email above.

8How to complain

To the Spanish data protection authority, AEPD: www.aepd.es.
